<p>
<strong>Niels Cariou-Kotlarek</strong> is a PhD student in group funded by the EPSRC. He is interested in stochastic processes, machine learning methods, and financial mathematics. His work primarily explores the use of jump-stochastic processes for simulating and generating financial as well as other related data sets. A key component of his research involves signature feature extraction techniques that allow for the effective representation and analysis of sequential data.
